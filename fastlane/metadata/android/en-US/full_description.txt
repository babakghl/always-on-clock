Always On Clock turns your Android phone into a proper nightstand or desk clock: big readable time, date, battery and a live weather forecast — with the customisation to make it yours.

CLOCK
• Digital or analog face
• 12 or 24-hour format, optional seconds
• Date line and battery level with charging indicator
• Clock size, 12 colours, 6 background colours, 6 fonts, bold toggle, in-app brightness

WEATHER
• Forecast for 1–7 days, powered by Open-Meteo (free, no API key needed) or OpenWeatherMap (your own key)
• Toggle each item: current temperature, condition, daily high, daily low, precipitation chance
• City search or device location
• Celsius or Fahrenheit, adjustable refresh interval
• Icon styles: emoji, simple symbols, or none

DISPLAY & BEHAVIOUR
• Screen-saver (Daydream) mode: set Always On Clock as your screen saver and Android starts it automatically when the phone is charging or docked — the closest an app can get to replacing the built-in always-on display
• Charging-only mode: the clock shows only while plugged in and exits when unplugged
• Burn-in protection: the clock face drifts by an adjustable distance (0–60 dp) at an adjustable interval (30 seconds to 5 minutes)
• Orientation lock: auto, portrait or landscape
• Keep-screen-on toggle

BACKGROUND
• A persistent status notification shows the clock is running, with the current time and weather, and keeps the forecast up to date

SETTINGS
• Live preview at the top of the Settings screen
• Options grouped in collapsible categories: Clock, Appearance, Weather, Display & Behaviour, Background

No ads. No tracking. No account. Open source under the MIT license.
