Everything a bedside clock should do

Built because phone makers' always-on displays won't show a real forecast, and most clock apps want a subscription. This one doesn't.

🌦️

Real weather forecast

1–7 days from Open-Meteo — free, no API key. Toggle current temp, condition, high, low and precipitation chance. City or device location, °C or °F.

💤

Takes over while charging

Set it as your Android screen saver and it appears automatically when the phone idles on the charger — the closest an app can get to replacing the built-in AOD.

🔌

Charging-only mode

The clock shows only while plugged in and politely exits when you unplug. Exactly how a nightstand clock should behave.

🛡️

Burn-in protection

The clock face drifts by an amount and interval you choose (0–60 dp, every 30 s to 5 min), so no pixel sits lit all night.

🎛️

Deeply customisable

Digital or analog, 12 colours, 6 fonts, size, brightness, orientation lock, icon styles — with a live preview in Settings and tidy collapsible categories.

🔔

You can see it's alive

A status notification shows the clock is running in the background, with the current time and weather — and keeps the forecast fresh.

Make it your always-on display

Android doesn't let apps replace the built-in AOD directly — the screen saver route below is the supported way, and it takes about a minute.

1

Install the APK

Download it from GitHub Releases and allow installs from your browser once.

2

Open the app's Settings

Tap the ⚙ on the clock, then “Open screen saver settings” in Display & Behaviour.

3

Pick Always On Clock

Select it as your screen saver and set it to start while charging.

4

Plug in and relax

When the screen times out on the charger, your clock takes over.

Private by design

🚫 No ads🕵️ No tracking👤 No account📖 Open source (MIT)
